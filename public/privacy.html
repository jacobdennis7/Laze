Laze — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Laze is a scheduling tool that maps your calendar. It is built so that your data stays with you.

What Laze accesses

With your permission, Laze reads your Google Calendar using Google's read-only scope (calendar.readonly). Laze cannot create, edit, or delete calendar events. When Laze helps you create an event, it opens Google Calendar's own interface prefilled — the saving happens in Google's product, under your account.

Where your data lives

Laze has no server and no database. Your calendar events, sign-in token, home/office addresses, and favorite spots are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to us, and we could not read it if we wanted to. Clearing your browser data removes everything.

Third-party services

Your browser talks directly to: Google (Calendar API, and optionally the Places and Routes APIs for nearby spots and live travel times), OpenStreetMap services (map tiles via CARTO, geocoding via Nominatim, places via Overpass). Each receives only what is necessary to answer the request (for example, map coordinates), governed by their own privacy policies.

Google API disclosure

Laze's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke Laze's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
