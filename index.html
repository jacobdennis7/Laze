Laze

Your week, on a map

Laze is a scheduling assistant. It connects to your Google Calendar with read-only access and places every meeting on a map, so you can see your day the way you'll actually move through it: travel time between back-to-back meetings, conflict warnings before they burn you, nearby cafés and restaurants for your next meeting, and suggestions for when and where to meet when someone asks for time.

Your calendar data is processed entirely in your browser and is never stored on a server.
